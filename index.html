Shubham Singh

Research Scientist at UChicago Data Science Institute

5460 S University Ave

Chicago, Illinois 60615

I am a Research Scientist at the UChicago Data Science Institute, working with Moon Duchin. I recently earned my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Illinois Chicago, advised by Chris Kanich and Ian A. Kash. My research sits at the intersection of algorithmic fairness, security and privacy, and computational social science, with a focus on diagnosing and mitigating biases in sociotechnical systems.

Previously, I led Working Groups for the EAAMO Bridges (formerly MD4SG) initiative, and I developed my research foundation at Precog Research Lab under Ponnurangam Kumaraguru “PK” and Arun Balaji Buduru. My industry background includes serving as a Full-Stack Developer at an Amazon–acquired startup and a software engineering intern at MobiKwik. I completed my undergraduate degree in Computer Science at IIIT-Delhi.
